Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 18, 2026

GeoAlarm ("the App") is developed by Elias Schlie. This policy explains how the App handles your data.

Summary

GeoAlarm does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything stays on your device.

Location Data

The App uses your device's location services to provide geofencing functionality. Specifically:

  • Your location is used to monitor circular geographic regions (geofences) that you define when creating alarms.
  • Location data is processed entirely on your device by iOS Core Location services.
  • No location data is ever sent to any server, third party, or external service.
  • The App requests "Always" location permission so that geofence monitoring works when the App is in the background. You can revoke this permission at any time in iOS Settings.

Data Storage

Alarm data (times, labels, locations, and preferences) is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. This data is not backed up to any external server. It may be included in your standard iCloud or iTunes device backups, which are governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Third-Party Services

The App uses Apple MapKit for map display and location search. Search queries are sent to Apple's servers and are subject to Apple's Privacy Policy. GeoAlarm itself does not operate any servers or use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Data Collection

The App does not collect:

  • Personal information (name, email, phone number)
  • Device identifiers or fingerprints
  • Usage analytics or telemetry
  • Crash reports (beyond what Apple collects via its own opt-in diagnostics)
  • Advertising data

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised date. Since the App collects no data, material changes are unlikely.
